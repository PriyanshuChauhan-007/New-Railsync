## 3. What the Video Demonstrates
1. **Problem Context**: Siloed scheduling between Civil Engineering, Electrical (TRD 25kV OHE), and Signalling (S&T), leading to unnecessary track closures and train delays.
2. **Mathematical Solution**: Real-time multi-objective constraint satisfaction via Google OR-Tools CP-SAT.
3. **Network Operations Center (NOC) UI**: Visualizing high-density corridors (Eastern Railway, Western Railway, Northern Railway).
4. **Time-Distance Diagram**: Demonstration that train headways and passenger services remain completely unhindered.
5. **Scenario Lab Dynamic Recovery**: Injecting a 35-minute train delay or emergency maintenance demand and observing sub-second re-optimization with minimum change to existing operations.
